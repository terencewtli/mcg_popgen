# E1: the passenger-methylation footprint

Code: `scripts/slim/e1_passenger.slim`, `scripts/python/{model,oracle,e1,e1_plot}.py`. Results: `csv/e1/`,
`pdf/e1/e1_passenger.pdf`. The model is specified exactly in `pdf/theory.pdf` (§3–6, 9).

**Question.** Methylation never enters fitness. How much CpG enrichment appears around a selected TF motif purely
because TF-protected (unmethylated) CpGs escape deamination?

**Design.** 300-bp locus, N = 100 (Q = 2), α = 2e-5, r = 20, burn-in 5/u, then 2/u of sampling. One heterozygous motif
mismatch has 2Ns = 10. Replicates: 40 per main condition, 20 per sweep. The oracle is exact for neutral M0_*; for P_* it
freezes the motif. Run 2026-10-01; about 25 min on 9 cores.

## Results

CpG density is per 100 bp. "Near" is < 25 bp from the motif edge and "far" is ≥ 75 bp. Contrast = near / far, using a
ratio of pooled means with a bootstrap SE.

| condition | near | far | contrast (SLiM) | oracle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0_ctx (neutral, context-only) | 0.96 | 0.94 | 1.02 ± 0.06 | 0.99 |
| M0_meth (neutral, gated) | 0.98 | 0.94 | 1.04 ± 0.06 | 0.97 |
| M0_meth_fb (+ density feedback) | 0.94 | 1.02 | 0.92 ± 0.06 | 1.03 |
| P_ctx (TF selected, context-only) | 0.93 | 0.92 | 1.01 ± 0.06 | 0.99 |
| **P_meth (TF selected, gated)** | **2.35** | **0.91** | **2.58 ± 0.16** | **2.44** |
| P_meth_fb | 2.84 | 0.90 | 3.16 ± 0.29 | 2.91 |
| P_meth λ = 10 / 50 | 1.71 / 2.93 | 0.86 / 0.95 | 2.00 / 3.09 | 1.55 / 3.25 |
| P_meth r = 5 / 50 | 4.47 / 1.04 | 2.87 / 0.43 | 1.56 / 2.44 | 1.57 / 3.09 |
| P_meth Q = 1 / 4 (N = 200 / 50) | 2.57 / 2.51 | 1.06 / 0.97 | 2.42 / 2.59 | 2.42 / 2.53 |

1. **A selected TF leaves a CpG footprint only when mutation is methylation-gated.** P_ctx shows no enrichment
   (1.01), while P_meth shows 2.6× within 25 bp and about 3.6× within 10 bp. No CpG is under selection in any run.
   The two-state prediction (theory §9: ~5× at the edge, 2–3× averaged over 25 bp) is right to within the accuracy of
   that approximation.
2. **The footprint scales with the protection range λ and with r.** It saturates at high r because the far flank
   empties too: at r = 50 there are 0.43 CpGs per 100 bp far from the motif.
3. **With a₁ = 0.5 and W = 25, density feedback does not make islands self-sustaining.** M0_meth_fb has no island once
   the TF is gone. Feedback amplifies a TF-seeded island by about 20% (3.16 vs 2.58, roughly 1.8 SE).
   Bistability would need stronger feedback; this is untested.
4. **Rescaling holds with selection on a sequence-encoded trait.** Q = 1, 2, 4 agree (|z| ≤ 0.8).
5. **Validation.** SLiM and Python methylation agree to ≤ 5e-7 across all 360 runs. Neutral SLiM matches the exact
   oracle (|z| ≤ 1.2) except one far-flank z = 2.49 (M0_meth_fb).

**Open item.** Three of 24 SLiM-vs-oracle z-scores exceed 2 (about 1 expected): M0_meth_fb far 2.49, λ = 10 far −2.15,
r = 50 near −2.26. The r = 50 contrast (2.44 vs 3.09) is the one to chase. The candidate explanation is the
frozen-motif approximation at a high CpG mutation supply, 4N·rα = 0.4. A Q-check at r = 50 would separate this from
noise.

**What it means.** Across species, CpG retention at TF-bound sites is predicted without any selection on CpGs or on
methylation. A conservation score that assumes one CpG mutation rate would read it as constraint. This is the
mechanism of Cohen et al. 2011, rebuilt forward with an explicit TF layer.
